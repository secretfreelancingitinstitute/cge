Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about visa sponsorship, verification, and global career opportunities

How does visa sponsorship verification work?

Every job posting with visa sponsorship is verified through our multi-step process. We validate company authorization, check USCIS databases, and confirm legitimate sponsorship capacity before displaying the verified badge.

How secure is my personal information?

We use enterprise-grade encryption and comply with GDPR, CCPA, and international data protection standards. Your data is never sold, and all communications are secured with end-to-end encryption.

What makes JELLWOW different from other job platforms?

Our AI-powered verification system, real-time global job matching, and exclusive partnerships with Fortune 500 companies create opportunities you won't find elsewhere. Plus, our trust score system eliminates fraudulent listings.

Can I search for remote positions globally?

Absolutely! Our advanced filters include remote, hybrid, and location-specific options. You can search by time zones, visa requirements, and even currency preferences for salary transparency.

How do I know if a company is legitimate?

Every company on our platform undergoes KYC verification. Look for our Trust Score (1-100), verified badges, and detailed company profiles with real employee reviews and salary data.
