How JELLWOW Works

Step-by-step: job seekers, employers, and our verification & trust process.

For Job Seekers

  1. Create a profile and build resumes.
  2. Search and filter verified jobs across countries.
  3. Apply and track applications.

For Employers

  1. Register your company and upload a logo.
  2. Undergo verification and KYC checks.
  3. Publish jobs and use applicant tracking.
